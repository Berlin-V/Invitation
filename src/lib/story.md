# Berlin & Jerlin Ashika — Our Story

## Where it began: Bethlehem Metric Hr. Sec. School, Karungal
Berlin and Jerlin Ashika's story starts long before either of them knew it was a love story. They joined the same 3rd grade class at Bethlehem Metric Hr. Sec. School, Karungal, and even sat their entrance exam in the very same exam hall — two kids who didn't yet know they'd one day stand at the altar together.

## From 7th grade: classmates
By 7th grade, they had become classmates properly — sharing classrooms, school days, and years of growing up side by side, still with no idea what was quietly being written for them.

## 12th grade: the first real conversation
It wasn't until 12th grade that they actually spoke for the first time — in the physics lab, during a public lab exam. A simple beginning, in the most unexpected place, to something neither of them saw coming.

## May 4th: the first WhatsApp conversation
Their first real conversation happened over WhatsApp on May 4th. What started as messages soon turned into deep, late-night calls and conversations that stretched on for hours. Both of them were falling in love — quietly, separately, neither one saying it out loud yet.

## June 18th: the confession
Jerlin Ashika was the braver of the two — she was the first to confess her love to Berlin, on June 18th. From that day, what had been unspoken became real.

## Three years, one long-distance love
What followed were three years of long-distance love — no in-person meetings, just phone calls and messages carrying their relationship across the distance. Through it all — the fights, the misunderstandings, and the love that kept bringing them back to each other — their bond moved through every stage two people can go through: strangers → schoolmates → classmates → friends → crush → lovers → fiancés.

## April 4, 2026: engaged on Easter Sunday
On April 4, 2026 — Easter Sunday — Berlin and Jerlin Ashika got engaged, marking the moment their long-distance love became a promise for forever.

## December 10, 2026: the wedding
And now, a new role and a new beginning await — Berlin and Jerlin Ashika are getting married on December 10, 2026.
